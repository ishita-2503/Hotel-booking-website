About us

Jaipur is a reminder of the State’s legendary cuisine legacy, from the by-lanes of Old City, to the palatial spread of the iconic royal abodes; from the signature cuisine feasted upon by “Kalanders of Urs’’ at a “Musafir-Khana’’,to the contemporary global cuisine, THE COZY INN has a lot to offer.

Wellness, fitness and inner peace are gently promoted at THE COZY INN. Kaya Kalp – The Spa offers award-winning therapies. Alongside are professional services at Salon, serene swimming pool and the latest equipment in the high-power gym. Personalised attention awaits.
